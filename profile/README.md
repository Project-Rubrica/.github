## What we're building

Rubrica is a school management platform designed around competency-based education in Kenya. We are bringing lesson planning, assessment, learner records, parent communication and school operations into one connected experience.

## One login, your profiles

A teacher at one school. A parent at another. A driver or staff member elsewhere.

Sign in once, choose your school-and-role profile, and enter the workspace relevant to you. The app and web experience follow the same approach, with permissions and school data kept separate.

## Our focus

- **Teaching and learning:** planning, assessments, portfolios and progress reports.
- **School communities:** learners, teachers, families and staff working together.
- **School operations:** attendance, timetables, finance and transport, delivered in phases.

## Project status

Rubrica is under active development. Our current prototype supports design and workflow reviews using sample data; it is not a production service.

**Team members:** [Open the project repository](https://github.com/Project-Rubrica/rubrica) for the prototype, documentation and reference materials. Repository access requires an accepted team invitation.
